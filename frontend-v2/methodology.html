Data sources & methodology

A plain-language record of what the demo observes, calculates, detects, derives, simulates, and illustrates.

This is public benchmark data and is not Sime Darby Property operational data. The demo uses four Building Data Genome Project 2 whole-building electricity meters. It does not represent Elmina Lakeside Mall or a real township deployment.

Unsupported claims

BDG2 does not establish Sime Darby or Elmina asset identity, township coordinates, geometry, floors, rooms, equipment, HVAC causes, schedules, Malaysian tariffs, Malaysian carbon factors, measured solar or battery performance, interventions, or verified savings.

Data classifications used throughout the demo

Observed public benchmark data

Imported BDG2 readings. These are the only observed operational values displayed.

Calculated forecast

A 24-hour model output derived from BDG2 history, with held-out error metrics.

Detected unusual consumption pattern

A statistical residual detection. It never establishes HVAC, lighting, waste, ghost-load, or equipment causes.

Derived benchmark comparison

A comparison or aggregation calculated from the selected public benchmark buildings.

Simulated assumption

A user-entered or default hypothetical input and its result. It is not measured operational data.

Illustrative visual

A navigation or presentation graphic. It is not measured geometry or a verified digital twin.

Forecast and detection method

  • The backend trains and evaluates only on validated BDG2 readings with chronological splits.
  • The next 24 hourly values are calculated forecasts, compared with a previous-day same-hour baseline.
  • Unusual patterns use forecast residuals and a validation-derived MAD threshold. A detection is not a confirmed cause.

Simulation assumptions

  • The simulator uses the selected asset’s calculated 24-hour forecast as its demand input.
  • Solar follows a clearly labelled hypothetical normalized daytime curve (not measured irradiance); capacity is user-entered or a displayed default.
  • Battery capacity, charge/discharge limit, and starting state are simulated assumptions. Charge and discharge efficiency is assumed 95% per leg, with discharge targeting 85% of the baseline peak after solar. A sine-shaped solar profile uses the asset source timezone, 06:00–18:00 local.
  • Flexible load is shifted between user-selected forecast intervals. No live control action is performed.
  • No financial output is shown unless the user enters a price. Any result is labelled “Illustrative estimate based on user-entered tariff”.
  • One-hour interval kWh divided by one hour is labelled as derived interval-average kW, not instantaneous measured demand.

Peer comparison and Green CFO rules

  • Peer totals, mean interval-average kW, peak interval-average kW and kWh/m² use only timestamps present for all four BDG2 buildings. Floor area comes from BDG2 metadata. Different building uses, schedules and weather prevent a normalized efficiency ranking.
  • Overnight share means observed energy from 00:00–05:59 in each source-local timezone divided by the common-window total. The highest share is only a candidate for investigation; without schedules, occupancy or equipment mapping it is not confirmed ghost load, waste or a fault.
  • Green CFO inputs are entered by the user. Annual cost change = expected annual energy change × tariff. Annual carbon change = expected annual energy change × carbon factor. Annual net benefit = cost change − annual operating-cost change. Simple payback = CAPEX ÷ positive annual net benefit.
  • Every candidate requires CAPEX, expected annual energy change, tariff, carbon factor, operating-cost change and an assumption source. Missing inputs show “Not calculated”. Eligible scenarios are ordered by shortest simple payback, then annual net benefit, and labelled “Illustrative scenario ranking”.

Local demo calculation rules

Portfolio totals use only common observed timestamps. Storage uses solar-only charging, bounded power and capacity, no export credit, and explicit initial stored energy. Peak shifting moves entered kWh without changing total energy; a negative peak reduction indicates a worse peak. Flat-rate cost = interval kWh × entered MYR/kWh. Emissions = interval kWh × entered kg CO2e/kWh. Simple payback = entered CAPEX / (entered annual kWh reduction × entered rate − entered annual maintenance); positive net benefit and every input are required. No day is annualised; missing inputs show Not calculated. Scene geometry, photographs, solar shapes and equipment graphics are illustrative. The read-only AI panel sends your question and selected-building BDG2 evidence to Google Gemini only when you send. Optional simulation inputs are explicitly included and recomputed by the backend. Responses cite observed/forecast/detected/simulated evidence and may be incorrect. No tools, settings changes or equipment control are available. AI is unavailable without a privately configured backend provider; never enter secrets or personal data. All simulations are hypothetical and perform no live control.
